# Participant feedback from SSI RCM101 workshops and clinics (July 2025)

## What are your key take-away(s) from this workshop?

- very useful, concise yet informative session. 
- “Vibes and visibility” and “Documentation is our love language”
- "If you are doing it alone, you are doing it wrong"
- So much to do - so little time :-(
- I need to think more about strategy in my community.
- I didn't find much useful from the workshop
- Doing community management well is hard
- level of community participation + stages of community building are BOTH KEY to describe the maturity of a community

## Pluses: What was good, insightful or valuable from this session?

- discussions, even the chat ones. 
- The part about marketing and how it is connected to community work
- Really useful to have a working definition of RCM, the use of the RCM framework as a diagnostic tool for my community. 2 hours with a break is a good format. 
- Reminder that skills can and should be co-opted from the community to avoid dependence on one person driving the community.
- Some great metaphors: Documentation is love language, and RCMs are glue. I like the marketing tool/flipped marketing tool idea too! Pluses for friendly vibe, and interactivity
- The subsequent clinic was really useful, the discussions around people questions provided multiple actionable insights on topics relevant to my own difficulties.
- Some good steers about how to elevate community planning (thinking strategically)
- The community maturation indicator!


## Deltas: What did not go well, should be improved or was least valuable? 

- chocolate delivery for next session! 
- The Miro task felt a bit overwhelming, I did not feel comfortable in putting "I have this skills" with many things, because I am often doing them because no one else does them, but I find them important, but I do not think I am doing good job. Maybe there could be different stars for "I feel comfortable doing them" and "I can do it, but not comfortably"
- When given in different contexts, it would be useful to provide the Turing Way framing at the top of the workshop to frame the entire presentation. It's current place (right before the RCM definition) feels a bit disruptive to the flow.
- Nothing I can think of.
- maybe split the "skills" from the "resources" part in the questions where we assign stars?
- Half the 2 hour session seemed to be taken up by introductions and ice breakers, before introducing a slide with ~25 terms, many which could be interpreted in multiple ways, to be completed. The result of this activity wasn't then well resolved (or perhaps my attention had drifted by this point).
- struggled with Miro board, also need more space to think about these outside the session - some suggestions for tools for capturing these things other than Miro
- I felt that there was a lot of content in this session, and we had to go through it very fast
